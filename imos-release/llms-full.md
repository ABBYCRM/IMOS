**Indicative pricing:** $10 discount to Platts Mediterranean Benchmark. The same source document also describes this as "Rotterdam minus $10USD"; both wordings are shown as written and are not reconciled. The benchmark and final discount are subject to supplier compliance and a written quotation. This is not guaranteed pricing. Fixed term pricing may be available by mutual agreement.

**Compliance submission** (to truxenergygroup@gmail.com, as stated on the site):
1. Letter of Intent (LOI) addressed to TruxEnergy Group.
2. Complete Buyer CIS (Corporate Information Sheet).
3. Signed Acceptance Letter, accepting the enclosed procedure.
4. Proof of Funds: bank statement, or balance screenshot/photo, showing the buyer company name.

Banking documents are sensitive. Share them only after independently verifying the recipient and an approved secure process. Never send financial documents through the public website; it does not collect them.

**After successful compliance:**
1. TruxEnergy issues the final quotation and procedure.
2. Pro forma agreement is reviewed.
3. A signed agreement leads to a conference with the supplier, and supplier details are provided for the ICPO.
4. The CIF procedure then follows.

An SBLC may be considered if a DLC is unavailable, subject to a formal written request and approval.

**CIF procedure, ten steps:** 1) Buyer ICPO; 2) Seller draft contract; 3) Electronic signatures; 4) Lodge with banks; 5) DLC and performance bond; 6) Alternative to DLC; 7) Loading and inspection; 8) Arrival and payment; 9) Title transfer; 10) Monthly deliveries.
